**Day 1: Arrival in Bali**
* Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport in Bali (Cost: $500-$700 for flights from Bengaluru)
* Check-in at [Hotel Puri Santrian](https://www.purisandrian.com/) in Sanur, Bali (Cost: $80-$120 per night)
* Explore the Sanur Beach and its surroundings (Cost: Free)
* Enjoy a traditional Balinese dinner at a local restaurant (Cost: $10-$20 per meal)

**Day 2: South Bali Exploration**
* Visit the famous Uluwatu Temple (Cost: $10-$15 per person)
* Watch the Kecak Fire Dance at Uluwatu (Cost: $10-$15 per person)
* Explore the beautiful beaches of Nusa Dua (Cost: Free)
* Enjoy lunch at a local warung (Cost: $5-$10 per meal)
* Visit the Garuda Wisnu Kencana Cultural Park (Cost: $10-$15 per person)

**Day 3: Cultural Experiences**
* Take a cooking class to learn about Balinese cuisine (Cost: $30-$50 per person)
* Visit the Tirta Empul Temple and take a holy water bath (Cost: $10-$15 per person)
* Explore the Ubud Art Market (Cost: Free)
* Enjoy a traditional Balinese massage (Cost: $20-$30 per person)

**Day 4: Historical Sightseeing**
* Visit the Taman Ayun Temple in Mengwi (Cost: $10-$15 per person)
* Explore the Bedugul area and visit the Ulun Danu Beratan Temple (Cost: $10-$15 per person)
* Visit the Jatiluwih Rice Terraces (Cost: $10-$15 per person)
* Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant (Cost: $10-$20 per meal)

**Day 5: East Bali Adventure**
* Take a day trip to the Lempuyang Temple (Cost: $50-$70 per person)
* Explore the Candidasa area and visit the Tirta Gangga Water Palace (Cost: $10-$15 per person)
* Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant (Cost: $10-$20 per meal)
* Visit the Goa Lawah Temple (Cost: $10-$15 per person)

**Day 6: Departure**
* Spend the morning at leisure (Cost: Free)
* Check-out of the hotel and depart for the airport (Cost: $10-$20 for taxi)
* Depart from Ngurah Rai International Airport (Cost: $500-$700 for flights to Bengaluru)

**Budget Breakdown:**
* Flights from Bengaluru to Bali: $500-$700
* Accommodation for 5 nights: $400-$600
* Transportation: $50-$100
* Food and drinks: $200-$300
* Activities and entrance fees: $150-$250
* Total: $1300-$1650
