**Day 1: Arrival in Zurich**
* Arrive at Zurich Airport (Cost: $800-$1,200 for flights from Delhi)
* Check-in at [Hotel du Theatre](https://www.hoteldutheatre.com/) (Cost: $150-$200 per night)
* Explore the historic Old Town, including the Fraumünster Church and the Limmat River (Cost: Free)
* Enjoy traditional Swiss cuisine at a local restaurant, such as [Zeughauskeller](https://www.zeughauskeller.ch/) (Cost: $20-$50 per meal)

**Day 2: Zurich City Tour**
* Visit the Swiss National Museum (Cost: $10-$15 per person)
* Take a stroll along the Lake Zurich promenade (Cost: Free)
* Visit the Kunsthaus art museum (Cost: $15-$20 per person)
* Enjoy a traditional Swiss dinner at a local restaurant, such as [Rheinfelder Bierhalle](https://www.rheinfelder-bierhalle.ch/) (Cost: $30-$60 per meal)

**Day 3: Lucerne Day Trip**
* Take a train to Lucerne (Cost: $60-$100 per person, return)
* Visit the Chapel Bridge and the Lion Monument (Cost: Free)
* Explore the Old Town and its charming streets (Cost: Free)
* Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant, such as [Old Swiss House](https://www.oldswiss-house.ch/) (Cost: $20-$40 per meal)

**Day 4: Interlaken Adventure**
* Take a train to Interlaken (Cost: $60-$100 per person, one way)
* Check-in at [Hotel Metropole](https://www.metropole-interlaken.ch/) (Cost: $120-$200 per night)
* Enjoy outdoor activities, such as hiking or paragliding (Cost: $50-$100 per person)
* Enjoy dinner at a local restaurant, such as [Restaurant Schuh](https://www.restaurant-schuh.ch/) (Cost: $30-$60 per meal)

**Day 5: Jungfraujoch Excursion**
* Take a train to the Jungfraujoch, the "Top of Europe" (Cost: $100-$150 per person, return)
* Enjoy breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains and glaciers (Cost: Free)
* Visit the Ice Palace and the Sphinx Observatory (Cost: Free)
* Return to Interlaken and enjoy dinner at a local restaurant (Cost: $30-$60 per meal)

**Day 6: Geneva City Tour**
* Take a train to Geneva (Cost: $60-$100 per person, one way)
* Check-in at [Hotel des Trois Couronnes](https://www.hoteltroiscouronnes.com/) (Cost: $150-$250 per night)
* Visit the Palais des Nations and the Jet d'Eau fountain (Cost: Free)
* Explore the Old Town and its charming streets (Cost: Free)
* Enjoy dinner at a local restaurant, such as [Restaurant de l'Hôtel de Ville](https://www.restaurantdehoteldeville.ch/) (Cost: $40-$80 per meal)

**Day 7: Departure from Geneva**
* Spend the morning shopping for souvenirs or visiting any last-minute attractions (Cost: Free)
* Depart from Geneva Airport (Cost: $800-$1,200 for flights to Delhi)

**Budget Breakdown:**
* Flights from Delhi to Zurich and Geneva to Delhi: $1,600-$2,400
* Accommodation (6 nights): $1,200-$2,400
* Transportation (trains and buses): $300-$600
* Food and drink: $800-$1,200
* Attractions and activities: $300-$600
* Total: $4,200-$6,800
